---
title: "RWAToken"
description: "`RWAToken` is the core real-world asset (RWA) token contract in the ArtStar protocol, with comprehensive compliance controls and primary market sale management. It supports primary sales settled in USDT, signature-based compliance checks, purchase limits, and time-bounded sale windows."
---

<Note>
  **Upgradability**

This contract uses the `UUPSUpgradeable` pattern and inherits from `ERC20Upgradeable`, `OwnableUpgradeable`, and `PausableUpgradeable`.

</Note>
