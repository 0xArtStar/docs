Welcome to **ArtStar** — an RWA (Real World Asset) investment platform focused on tokenizing blue-chip artworks. With blockchain technology, you can invest in fractional ownership of world-class art in a secure and transparent way.

To help you get started quickly and avoid common pitfalls, this guide walks you through the full flow—from **connecting your wallet** to **completing your first purchase**.

<Card title="Prerequisites" icon="wallet">
  Before you begin, make sure you have a Web3 wallet (MetaMask recommended), enough **BEP-20 USDT**, and a small amount of **BNB**.
</Card>

## <Icon icon="download" /> Preparation: Install and Configure a Web3 Wallet

If you don’t have a Web3 wallet yet, we strongly recommend **MetaMask**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download & Install" icon="browser">
    - **Desktop**: Visit the [MetaMask website](https://metamask.io/) and install the official browser extension (Chrome or Edge recommended).
    - **Mobile**: Search for “MetaMask” in the Apple App Store or Google Play and install the official app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a Wallet & Back Up" icon="key">
    Open MetaMask and follow the on-screen instructions to create a new wallet.
    <Warning>
      **Critical**: Write down your 12-word **Secret Recovery Phrase** on paper and store it securely. Anyone with this phrase can control your funds—never take screenshots or share it with anyone.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the BNB Chain Network" icon="network-wired">
    ArtStar runs on **BNB Chain (BNB Smart Chain)**. When you connect your wallet on the website, you’ll typically be prompted to add and switch to the network automatically.

  ```javascript
  {
  networkName: "BNB Smart Chain",
  chainId: 56,
  rpcUrl: "https://bsc-dataseed.binance.org",
  symbol: "BNB",
  explorer: "https://bscscan.com"
  }
  ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

## <Icon icon="wrench" /> Step 1: Connect Your Wallet and Create an Account

On ArtStar, your Web3 wallet is your identity. There’s no traditional username/password—connecting your wallet is enough to register and sign in.

<Info>
  **Network & Asset Requirements**
  1. **Network**: All issuance and trading on ArtStar happens on **BNB Chain (formerly BSC)**.
  2. **Settlement asset**: Prepare **BEP-20 USDT** for purchases (do not use USDT on ERC-20/TRC-20 networks, or your funds may not appear and won’t be usable).
  3. **Gas fees**: Keep a small amount of **BNB** in your wallet (recommended at least **0.01 BNB**) to pay network fees. Without BNB, you won’t be able to complete on-chain actions.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Website" icon="globe">
    Open the ArtStar website homepage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Your Wallet" icon="link">
    Click **“Connect Wallet”** in the top-right corner. If you entered via an invitation link, click **“Connect and continue”**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Wallet & Network" icon="diagram-project">
    In the wallet selector, choose your preferred wallet (MetaMask/Trust Wallet recommended), or sign in with email to create a Privy embedded wallet—then **make sure the network is set to BNB Chain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign to Confirm" icon="signature">
    Complete the signature request in your wallet to confirm login.
    *(Note: this login signature only proves wallet ownership. It does not spend funds or consume gas.)*
  </Step>
</Steps>

## <Icon icon="shield-check" /> Step 2: Complete KYC Verification

To comply with global regulations and protect investors, you must complete KYC (Know Your Customer) verification before participating in any Launchpad purchase.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Verification" icon="user">
    Click your wallet address in the top-right corner to open the dropdown menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start KYC" icon="file-signature">
    Select **“KYC Verification”**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Information" icon="id-card">
    Follow the guided flow to submit your basic information and identity documents.
    *(Note: due to compliance requirements, users from certain restricted countries/regions may not be able to pass verification. Please follow the on-screen prompts.)*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Approval" icon="hourglass-half">
    After submission, the system will review your application. Once approved, you’ll see a green shield icon and an **“Approved / Whitelisted”** status in the menu.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only KYC-approved (whitelisted) users can purchase shares in the Launchpad.
</Note>

  <Step title="Review Key Information" icon="magnifying-glass-chart">

    - **Valuation Report**: third-party valuation, market trend analysis, and liquidity rating.

## <Icon icon="coins" /> Step 4: Approve and Purchase (Mint)

Once you’ve decided to invest, you can complete the purchase from the trading panel on the right side of the details page. The flow is always **two-step**: **Approve** first, then **Purchase**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm Your Status" icon="check-circle">
    In **Purchase Shares**, confirm you have enough BEP-20 USDT and that your whitelist (KYC) badge is visible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an Amount" icon="keyboard">
    Enter the USDT amount you want to invest (must meet the asset’s **minimum purchase requirement**). The system will calculate the **Shares** you will receive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 1: Approve USDT" icon="unlock">
    - If this is your first purchase (or your allowance is insufficient), click **Approve USDT**.
    - Your wallet will prompt you to approve the transaction, allowing the smart contract to spend the specified amount of USDT.
    - Wait for on-chain confirmation (typically 3–5 seconds).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2: Confirm Purchase" icon="cart-shopping">
    - After approval, the button changes to **Confirm Purchase** automatically.
    - Click it and confirm the final mint transaction in your wallet. Your USDT will be deducted and the shares tokens will be sent to your wallet.
    - Once completed, you’ll see a success message and your transaction hash.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Common Pitfall**
  
  **Approving is not the same as purchasing.** Approval only grants spending permission. You **must** click **Confirm Purchase** after approval and complete the second wallet confirmation to finalize the purchase.
</Warning>

## <Icon icon="circle-question" /> FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can’t I click “Confirm Purchase”, or why does my transaction fail?" icon="circle-xmark">
    Check the following in order:
    1. **Network**: Is your wallet on **BNB Chain (BSC)**?
    2. **Gas**: Do you have enough **BNB**? (USDT cannot be used to pay gas.)
    3. **Token network**: Are you using **BEP-20 USDT**? (If your USDT is on Ethereum/Tron, bridge or transfer it to BNB Chain first.)
    4. **Minimum amount**: Does your input meet the asset’s minimum purchase requirement?
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What should I do if the page is stuck due to a network error?" icon="wifi">
    Try refreshing the page or reconnecting your wallet from the top-right menu. If your transaction stays in “Pending” in your wallet, the network may be congested—wait a bit, or increase gas in your wallet to speed it up.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
